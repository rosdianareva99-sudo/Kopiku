ABOUT US

Welcome to Kopiku Café, a cozy place where every cup of coffee is made with care and every guest is welcomed like a friend.

As a newly established café, our goal is to create a warm and comfortable space for people to relax, study, work, or spend time with loved ones. We serve freshly brewed coffee, delicious matcha drinks, and homemade bakery items prepared with quality ingredients and passion.

At Kopiku Café, we believe that simple moments become special when shared over a good drink and a tasty treat.

We are committed to providing friendly service and creating memorable experiences for everyone who visits us.

VISION
